Maa Sakhada: The Eternal Guardian of Mithila.

Gemini said Founded by King Sakrasinha Dev (a descendant of King Nanyadeva), this temple began when a goddess statue was discovered while clearing the Saptari forests. Originally named Sakhreswari Bhagawati, the shrine has been a spiritual center for over 300 years. It is famous for the ancient tradition of goat sacrifices (bali) performed by thousands of devotees during Bada Dashain.
