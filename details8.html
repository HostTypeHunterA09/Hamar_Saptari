Shambhunath Temple is a prominent Shiva pilgrimage site in Saptari, famous for its ancient Shivalinga and 1996 reconstruction.

Shambhunath Temple is a premier Shiva pilgrimage site in Saptari, housing an ancient 6-foot Shivalinga from Chandrabhoga Gadhi. The temple anchors a massive month-long festival starting every Nepali New Year (April 14), drawing thousands of regional devotees. It is uniquely known for the tradition of offering brinjals to seek divine healing for skin ailments and physical warts. Perfectly situated near the East-West Highway, it serves as a vital spiritual and social landmark in the Madhesh Province.
