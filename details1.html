Maa Kankalini Temple is a sacred Hindu temple dedicated to Goddess Kankalini, a powerful form of Kali.

According to local belief, the temple was built long ago after people discovered a divine idol of the goddess at the site. Since then, devotees have visited the temple with faith and devotion, believing that the goddess protects them and fulfills their wishes.
