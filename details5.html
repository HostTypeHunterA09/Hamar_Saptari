Home / Hamar Saptari

A serene Chure foothill sanctuary in Saptari featuring natural springs, Bhimsen temple, and off-road trails.

Gemini said Dharapani’s Bhimsen Dham flows from ancient legends into the lush Chure foothills. The rugged "Pahad" geography provides a cool, forested escape from the Terai. Vibrant picnic groups and off-road bikers turn gravel riverbeds into high-energy playgrounds. Easily reached via Simra Chowk, it’s a hub for spiritual and trail-riding movement. This site uniquely blends Saptari’s sacred myths with untamed nature and adventure.
