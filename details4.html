Koshi Barrage: Nepal's Largest Multi-Purpose Dam and Iconic Scenic Landmark

Gemini said The Koshi Barrage View Tower offers breathtaking vistas of the massive 56-gate dam and iconic sunsets over the vast river expanse. This ecological haven serves as a gateway to Koshi Tappu, where rare Gangetic dolphins and migratory birds thrive in a protected sanctuary. Spirituality meets nature through the nearby Kankalini Temple, creating a seamless blend of cultural fusion for pilgrims and travelers alike. Visitors enjoy a deep local immersion, experiencing the river-based lifestyle of Madhesh while savoring fresh Koshi fish at vibrant riverside eateries.
