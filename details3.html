Hamar Saptari Details

Home / Hamar Saptari

A prominent roadside temple in Saptari featuring a massive black Lord Shani.

The serene environment is framed by the distant green Chure hills (Pahad), creating a scenic backdrop for the massive black deity. Adrenaline-seeking bikers find raw excitement on the rugged off-road "pahad roads" and gravel riverbeds branching north into the foothills. This unique location blends constant pilgrim movement with untamed trails, making it a perfect stop for both deep devotion and wild exploration.
