Rajbiraj's history spans from ancient mythological origins to its modern status as Nepal's first planned city.

Rajbiraj is Nepal’s first planned city, designed in 1938 based on Jaipur’s iconic grid-pattern model. Steeped in mythology, it was the ancient seat of King Kushadhwaja and the 8th-century Rajdevi Temple. It became the district headquarters in 1941 following devastating Koshi River floods in Hanumannagar. A historic revolutionary hub, the city famously sheltered and freed prominent Indian freedom fighters.
