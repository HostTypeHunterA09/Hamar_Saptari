Scenic Saptari lake featuring the sacred Thakur Baba Temple and vibrant boating.

Lohajara Daha is a stunning natural lake in Tirhut, Saptari, featuring the sacred Thakur Baba Temple at its center. Surrounded by protected forests, it serves as a peaceful ecological sanctuary for migratory birds and local wildlife. The area is a vibrant hub for leisure, where daily pilgrims and tourists enjoy boating and scenic picnic spots. Located 4 km south of Mahuli, it offers a raw rural experience through forest trails and traditional river-based lifestyles.
